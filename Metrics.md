# Additional metrics to consider

## After hours
This should be calculated by looking at the timespan from Dispatch to on station time. A normal shift is 0800 to 1800. Any time during the time span from dispatch to on station that falls outside 0800 to 1800 is considered after hours time. Express as hours to two decimal places.

## Clinical skills
Aggregate totals of clinical skills from multi select data source.

## Operational skills
Aggregate totals of operational skills.
Skills to track include, ACR, nappy winch, stretcher winch. These will be multi select so don't double count. For example if ia job is marked ACR and nappy winch it is still just one job.
